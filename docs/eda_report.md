_Test:_ Croston/TSB beat ETS on intermittent/lumpy series; a Tweedie or Poisson LightGBM objective beats L2 on them.

**H3.** All categories have a strong weekly cycle peaking at the weekend; peak/trough ratio ranges from 1.37 (FOODS) to 1.48 (HOUSEHOLD).
_Test:_ Seasonal naive (7) beats naive (1) at every horizon; calendar features give a material gain in ablation step 2.

_Test:_ Ablation step 3 (+SNAP) improves FOODS WRMSSE but not HOUSEHOLD/HOBBIES.

**H5.** 20,731 series contain 50,900 implausible 14–55-day zero runs (2.97% of active series-days), consistent with unobserved stockouts; a further 11.30% of active series-days sit in dormant runs of 56+ days, which are kept as real demand regimes.
